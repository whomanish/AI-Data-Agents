# Changelog

## 0.1.0-preview.1

- First installable preview for turning product-usage questions into defined
  cohorts, reviewable audience decisions, and campaign and measurement
  handoffs.
- Supports product-usage campaign advice, ambiguity clarification, and a local
  synthetic cohort-to-audience workflow.
- Keeps identity, eligibility, suppression, holdout, unresolved records, and
  external-action authorization explicit.
- Includes a blank organization-overlay template, deterministic local control
  and audience-pipeline tools, schemas, and implementation references.
- Supports controlled local non-sending demonstrations only. Production data
  and external campaign actions remain unsupported.
- Adds reproducible packaging, a content manifest, checksums, an offline smoke
  checker, and explicit in-package preview labeling.
