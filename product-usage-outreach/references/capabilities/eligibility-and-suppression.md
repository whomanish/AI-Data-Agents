# Eligibility and suppression

Establish the authoritative rules and precedence for consent, suppression, internal or test status, account state, geography, entitlement, frequency, and channel-specific exclusions. Record which decisions are technical evidence and which require an authorized human policy owner.

Unknown consent, suppression, entitlement, account, or policy evidence never permits inclusion. Exclude or hold unresolved records under confirmed policy, and stop readiness when the policy itself is ambiguous. Never infer legal or regulatory treatment.

Ready evidence includes authoritative authorities and access, confirmed precedence and scope, explicit unknown dispositions, validation against controlled output, provenance, policy owner, version, and freshness. Email requires confirmed contactability and suppression. In-app requires entitlement and applicable exposure or frequency constraints, without importing email contactability assumptions.
