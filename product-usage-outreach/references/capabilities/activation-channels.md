# Activation channels

Establish the requested channel's target contract, required identifier, permissible audience fields, upload or activation operation, output validation, and handoff path. Treat a channel connector's read, export, and write operations independently.

If a target identifier, channel requirement, or write capability is unknown, produce only the highest safe local artifact or an approved handoff. Do not upload, publish, schedule, send, or activate until the user confirms the specific target, action, audience count, and unresolved items immediately beforehand.

Ready evidence includes channel source authority, operation tests, identifier and field contract, unknown handling, output validation, provenance, operating scope, and freshness. Email requires the approved contact projection and suppression outcome. In-app requires product identity, entitlement, exposure, and frequency evidence; it does not require email fields unless the requested channel contract does.
