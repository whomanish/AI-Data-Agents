# Usage signals

Establish the authoritative behaviour source, event or query meaning, timestamp and population semantics, supported filters, freshness, and a reproducible cohort contract. Validate thresholds, windows, exclusions, uniqueness, and plausible aggregate size before full enrichment.

If an event's meaning, coverage, or population is unknown, keep records unresolved or stop the affected cohort transition; do not infer adoption, non-adoption, or exposure from a convenient event name. Use metadata, aggregates, and stratified minimal samples before row-level inspection.

Ready evidence includes source and access, semantic confirmation, supported population and range, configuration or query fingerprint, unknown disposition, validation results, provenance, and freshness. Email and in-app can share a behavioural cohort, but in-app may additionally require exposure or frequency evidence before targeting.
