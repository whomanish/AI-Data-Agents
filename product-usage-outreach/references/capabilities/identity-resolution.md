# Identity resolution

Establish the authoritative identifiers, join path, account or workspace boundary, collision handling, known population scope, and traceable output contract. Measure candidate join coverage by relevant strata and validate that each accepted join maps to the intended person and workspace.

Missing, ambiguous, conflicting, or cross-person identity evidence is unresolved or excluded under confirmed policy; never select a likely match. Keep identifiers and row-level joins in controlled campaign files, and retain only non-PII evidence summaries in the overlay.

Ready evidence includes source authority, access, semantic confirmation, tested mapping and validation, collision and unknown dispositions, supported population, provenance, owner, and freshness. Email commonly needs an identity path to a contact authority. In-app needs the stable product target and workspace/account boundary, even when no email address exists.
