# Measurement capability

Establish the authoritative outcome signals, attribution or exposure evidence, observation window, holdout method, reporting contract, and owners for interpretation. Link them to the confirmed campaign behaviour and requested channel.

If outcome, exposure, or attribution semantics are unknown, do not claim measured impact; retain the campaign as a local plan or request a human decision. Keep recipient-level results and raw analytics in the campaign workspace, not in profiles or durable organization state.

Ready evidence includes source authority and access, semantic confirmation, holdout and reporting configuration, validation and reconciliation results, supported scope, provenance, owner, freshness, and operating ranges. Email commonly needs send or delivery and outcome evidence; in-app needs exposure and frequency evidence plus the outcome signal. Both use deterministic holdout and disposition-derived funnel reconciliation.
