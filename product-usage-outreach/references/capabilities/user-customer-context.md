# User and customer context

Establish the authoritative user, account, workspace, plan, role, lifecycle, and relevant ownership context needed by the campaign contract. Define the permitted joins, update timing, and context fields that affect eligibility or measurement.

Unknown or conflicting context does not become a permissive classification. Hold or exclude the affected record when context is material; record optional missing enrichment separately. Do not copy customer rows or personal data into organization knowledge.

Ready evidence includes authoritative source and access, field meaning, supported scopes, join validation, unknown handling, configuration fingerprint, provenance, and freshness. Email may need account ownership or customer-state context to decide who is contactable; in-app often needs workspace, role, or product-access context for the target experience.
