# Measurement and holdout design

Use this reference when advice or an operational campaign needs success criteria, measurement, or a holdout.

Define the primary outcome, observation window, eligible population, exclusions, exposure treatment, holdout method, and decision rule before activation-ready execution. Keep measurement definitions aligned with the confirmed cohort behaviour and channel. A change that affects inclusion, exposure, or outcome semantics requires targeted reassessment.

Use deterministic holdout assignment and retain its configuration, counts, and reasoned exclusions in campaign artifacts. Keep per-run recipients and results in the campaign workspace. Store only non-PII health summaries, operating ranges, fingerprints, and verified evidence references in the overlay.

Record funnel counts from final dispositions and reconcile them exactly. Record the practical control-path measurements available in the runtime: references loaded, questions, scripts or tools, latency, fallbacks, and mode transitions. A clean repeat profile should avoid cold bootstrap material and a full evaluation suite. Inspect or diagnose only when a quick check, mismatch, or user request requires it.
